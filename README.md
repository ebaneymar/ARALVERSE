# ARALVERSE

## Bayanihan Boss

Open **Start Game → Bayanihan Boss**, enter the number of pupils answering, and choose the difficulty. The boss uses the selected lesson’s built-in questions or your imported questions.

Project each question and have pupils hold up the letter of their answer. Select **Ipakita ang tamang sagot**, count the pupils holding up the correct letter, and enter the count under **Ilang bata ang tama?**. Select **Ilapat ang atake**. Each correct pupil deals 10 damage, so 12 correct pupils take 120 HP off the boss. Enter 0 if nobody was correct. Choose **Susunod na tanong** when the class is ready.

## Tournament players

The schoolyard match shows each name, rank badge and live HP in the upper corners. Reward counters sit beside the players, with the question, countdown and answer buttons below.

Use **♂ Lalaki** or **♀ Babae** on each roster card to choose that student’s standard uniform. Each choice is saved for that player. A new roster entry starts with neither icon selected.

Each active player appears as a full-body character using the photo saved under their roster name. Open **Tournament → Iayos ang mukha** on a player to preview the face, select a uniform, or use **Ilagay ang 5 punto** to fine-tune the fit. Choose **Unipormeng pambabae · blusa at palda** for the skirt uniform. **Ayusin ang laki at puwesto ng ulo** adjusts the head against the collar while previewing the finished character. The five points are the eye on the left of the photo, the eye on the right, the tip of the nose, the middle of the mouth, and the bottom of the chin.

To use a private class photo pack, open **Mga larawan ng klase → Buksan ang photo pack**, choose the matching picture, and press **I-save**. The pack stays available while Tournament is open. A photo is assigned only to the selected roster entry. Select **Photo** on the roster card to use an individual image.

Photos are processed on the classroom device. A player without a usable photo appears as a neutral silhouette until the teacher adds or aligns a photo.

Choose **Tournament skins** on a roster card to see that player's Avengers Series collection. Each tournament match medal unlocks the next skin in poster order, from Iron Guard at one win to Ant-Might at ten. Select an unlocked skin to wear its illustrated avatar in matches. The roster's Lalaki / Babae choice selects its artwork. **Gamitin ang uniporme** restores the player's uniform and fitted roster photo.

## Moving tournament panels in Layout

Drag either player's HP bar to move its name, rank emblem, team tab and gold plate together. To edit a single piece, enable **Select small parts inside an object**. For other combinations, Ctrl-click (Command-click on Mac), or enable **Multi-select objects** and choose objects on the scene or from the list. Drag any selected object, use the arrow keys, or set X and Y to move the selection. Choose **Save layout** to keep the positions.
