# Pet-feeding artwork

Nina follows the user's story illustrations: a Filipino girl with a yellow
short-sleeved shirt, blue knee-length shorts, a black low ponytail with a yellow
bow, and brown sandals. The game sprite shows her from behind. Nina is one
selectable character in the reusable **Pakainin ang mga Alaga** mode; the mode
also accepts other named characters supplied by the teacher.

Both final assets were created with the built-in imagegen tool. The original
PNG outputs are preserved. The game uses WebP encoding with alpha for offline
distribution; no background removal or character redesign was done in code.

Files: `nina-back-1582.webp` and `pet-feeding-1582.webp`.

## Final character prompt

Use the two Nina story illustrations as character-appearance references. Draw
only the little girl Nina, preserving her plain yellow T-shirt, blue knee-length
shorts, brown strapped sandals, black low ponytail, and yellow bow. Match the
warm outlined 2D storybook illustration style. Show her entire body and both
feet in a three-quarter rear view facing screen-right, with no visible face,
in a balanced friendly ready pose with empty hands slightly raised. Use genuine
alpha transparency and generous padding. No scarf, armor, vest, backpack,
sneakers, weapons, scene, basket, other characters, card, labels, or text.

Reference images: *Nina helps Lola carry the vegetable basket.png* and
*Nina and Nanay Pass Mang Inasal.png*.

## Final pet-atlas prompt

Create one 1536-by-1024 transparent sprite atlas with three equal columns and
two equal rows. Column one is a small golden-brown puppy with floppy ears and
a white chest. Column two is a cuddly brown owl. Column three is a gentle gray
carabao calf. Top-row animals look mildly hungry and sad; the matching animals
in the bottom row are happy and well-fed. Keep each whole animal inside its
512-by-512 cell with transparent margins, consistent identity, scale, and
baseline, facing slightly left. Match the warm outlined 2D Nina story style.
No aggression, sharp teeth, scene, props, ground, text, borders, or extra animals.
